Measures the latency of another MariaDB server or any other host.
`example_ping` sends one ping. `example_ping_stats` runs a series of pings
and returns minimum, maximum, average and standard deviation. Requires the
`ping3` module.

Source: [examples/ping]({{< repo "examples/ping" >}}).
